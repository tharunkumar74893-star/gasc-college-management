About GASC

Government Arts and Science College, Melvengatapuram provides undergraduate learning opportunities in arts, science and commerce. The college aims to support academic growth, responsible citizenship and a vibrant campus experience.

Our Vision

To create a centre of academic excellence and social responsibility.

Our Mission
